Kurdistan ATM

A free app for finding cash machines across the Kurdistan Region — with cash availability reported by the people using them.

What it does

  • Shows the nearest machines on a map or as a ranked list
  • Filters by bank and by city
  • Tells you whether a machine has cash — from user reports
  • Routes you there inside the app
  • ATM locations are bundled in the app, so the map works offline

Why the reports can be trusted

You can only submit a report when you are within 100 metres of the machine. That way every piece of information comes from someone who was actually standing there.

Free, with no adverts

No account is required, there are no adverts, and no personal data is collected.
